Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) Initiative

Empowering Traditional Indian Handloom Weavers & Artisans

For centuries, India's handloom weavers and traditional craftspeople have created world-renowned textiles and crafts. However, multiple layers of intermediaries, brokers, and predatory trade markups have traditionally absorbed the vast majority of profit margins.

No Intermediaries

Eliminates wholesale middlemen markups that dilute artisan earnings.

100% Fair Value

Revenues go directly to artisan studios and weaving families.

GI & Authenticity

Genuine pit loom and master craftsmanship verified at source.

Technical Project Highlights
  • Backend Architecture: Clean 3-tier Spring Boot MVC architecture (Controller → Service → Repository → JdbcTemplate → MySQL).
  • Pure JdbcTemplate: Free of heavy ORMs (No JPA, No Hibernate), providing direct, transparent, and high-performance SQL control.
  • Security & RBAC: Role-based access control with BCrypt password hashing for Customers, Artisans, and Administrators.
  • Transactional Integrity: Complete ACID transaction guarantee during checkout (Stock check → Order creation → Stock decrement → Payment record → Cart clearing).
